## 🎯 Mission
BHARATconnect is a social networking platform designed specifically for Indians, with strong emphasis on:
- **Fair Justice System**: AI + Human review for all actions
- **1-Hour Appeal Window**: Users can appeal account suspensions with evidence
- **Identity Verification**: Real ID proof to prevent fake accounts & scammers
- **Creator Economy**: Safe platform for Indians to earn money legitimately
- **Device-Level Security**: Hackers & repeat offenders face device bans
- **Zero Sexual Content**: Family-friendly environment
- **No Scams**: Strict anti-scam enforcement

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. **Dual Moderation System**
- 🤖 **AI Algorithm**: Automatic content detection (24/7)
- 👥 **Human Brain**: Manual review by team (for appeals)

### 2. **Account Suspension & Appeal**
- Auto-suspend for violations
- Users can provide evidence (screenshots, proofs)
- Team reviews within 1 hour
- Fair decision making

### 3. **Identity Verification**
- Government ID proof required
- Prevents fake accounts
- Business accounts need verification
- Hacker protection layer

### 4. **Fair Creator Economy**
- Creators earn legitimately
- Business verification system
- Payment integration
- Growth analytics

### 5. **Smart Reporting System**
- Users report with full evidence
- Automatic flagging system
- Team investigation
- Transparent outcomes
